Research

  • Isolating Driver Skill in Formula 1 ↗

    A Bayesian hierarchical model that separates a driver's true pace from the car beneath them, across the 2022–2025 ground-effect era. 64,745 racing laps, validated against teammate head-to-heads (r = 0.92). The headline: Verstappen is a class of one.

    Bayesian modeling · PyMC · 2022–2025 data

  • The Microeconomics of Modern War ↗

    A data-backed argument that cheap autonomous systems are inverting the cost-exchange ratios of modern war — a few-hundred-dollar FPV drone trading against a multi-million-dollar tank — and what that means for force design and procurement.

    Defense economics · Cost-exchange analysis · Sourced dataset
