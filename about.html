About Me

I grew up in Austin, Texas, and have spent the last several years working to understand how emerging technologies reshape national security and global competition. I studied business management with a concentration in engineering and analytics at MIT, and I'm currently finishing an MPhil in Technology Policy at the University of Cambridge.

I've worked across consulting, tech, and defense, from advising Fortune 500 clients at BCG to uncovering cloud security vulnerabilities at Microsoft to leading AI/ML research for the U.S. Air Force and Space Force at MIT. I plan to start full-time in consulting later this year.

Interests

I'm drawn to the space where technology, security, and strategy overlap, how new platforms and systems change the balance of power, how governments adapt to technological change, and where commercial innovation and military capability intersect. I also follow markets and investing closely, particularly where technology and geopolitics collide.

Outside of work, I'm fascinated by biohacking and human performance optimization. I played four years of varsity basketball at MIT and captained the team my senior year. These days I'm more likely watching F1, training MMA, or lifting. I like building things, whether it's model airplanes, side projects, or whatever I'm tinkering with at the time.
