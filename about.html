About Me

Consultant at BCG · MIT & Cambridge

I grew up in Austin and have spent the last several years on one question: how emerging technology reshapes national security and global competition. I studied business, engineering, and analytics at MIT, then read Technology Policy as an MPhil at Cambridge.

What pulls me in is the overlap of technology, security, and strategy — how new platforms shift the balance of power, how governments adapt (or don't), and where commercial innovation meets military capability. I follow markets closely, especially where technology and geopolitics collide. Today I'm a consultant at BCG.
