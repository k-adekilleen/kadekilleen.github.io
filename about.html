02About

Field notes.

I grew up in Austin, studied business, engineering, and analytics at MIT, and completed an MPhil in Technology Policy at Cambridge. I now work at an early-stage maritime logistics startup that is still in stealth.

I’m most interested in how new technology changes the way goods, capital, and power move around the world: autonomous systems at sea, fragile supply chains, and the point where commercial innovation becomes military capability.

I like to work through these questions by building things, from small models to longer research pieces.
