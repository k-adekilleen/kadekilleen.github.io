About Me

I grew up in Austin, Texas, and have spent the last several years working to understand how emerging technologies reshape national security and global competition. I studied business management with a concentration in engineering and analytics at MIT, and I'm currently finishing an MPhil in Technology Policy at the University of Cambridge.

I'm drawn to where technology, security, and strategy overlap — how new platforms shift the balance of power, how governments adapt to technological change, and where commercial innovation meets military capability. I follow markets closely, particularly where technology and geopolitics collide, and I'll be starting full-time in consulting later this year.
