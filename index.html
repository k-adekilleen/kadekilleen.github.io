Kade Killeen.

I work at an early-stage maritime logistics startup and write about defense, technology, and global security.

Scroll 30°16′N 97°44′W Move through the water

I grew up in Austin, captained the varsity basketball team at MIT while studying business, engineering, and analytics, and went on to an MPhil in Technology Policy at Cambridge. Along the way I’ve worked on defense strategy, supply chains, and cloud security, and today I’m at an early-stage maritime logistics startup.

My research follows one thread: how goods, capital, and power move around the world. I come at it from three directions: maritime logistics, defense economics, and emerging technology.
