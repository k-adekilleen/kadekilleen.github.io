Isolating Driver Skill in Formula 1

Bayesian modeling · PyMC · 2022–2025 data

In Formula 1, the car usually matters more than the driver. This project uses a Bayesian hierarchical model to separate a driver's true pace from the car beneath them, across the 2022–2025 ground-effect era.

The model is fit on 64,745 racing laps and validated against teammate head-to-heads (r = 0.92). The headline: Verstappen is a class of one.
