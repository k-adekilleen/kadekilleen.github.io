The Microeconomics of Modern War

  • Defense economics
  • Cost-exchange analysis

§ Summary

Cheap autonomous systems are inverting the cost-exchange ratios of modern war. A few-hundred-dollar FPV drone now trades against a multi-million-dollar tank.

This project builds a sourced dataset of these pairings and argues what the shift means for force design and procurement.
